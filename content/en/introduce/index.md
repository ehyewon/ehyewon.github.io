### My Thoughts

When I first started building this website, I was completely lost — but as I kept going, I found joy in designing it myself, and it turned into one of the best project experiences I’ve ever had.  
Since GPT came out, technology has been advancing at an incredible pace. I know it’ll take a lot of effort to make a living in this field, but I still want to give it a shot.  
Maybe there’s at least *one* thing I’m better at than AI… (please).  
Honestly, I just want to win the lottery and spend my days watching Reels.  
I hope everyone achieves their goals and dreams.  

What I always remind myself is this: *There is no such thing as being left behind in life.*  
Even if I seem slower than others, or feel like I’ve just come to my senses — life is full of unexpected turns.  
In the end, when we reach our 50s or 60s, we all end up in a similar place (as long as we keep trying).  
As long as we stay healthy and alive, that’s enough.  
So please, love yourself, believe that you can do it, don’t put yourself down, stay confident, and remember — most things aren’t as big as they seem.  

We’re nothing more than tiny specks of cosmic dust, smaller than dust itself.  
So instead of digging into endless worries and making yourself miserable, focus on the present and find small moments of happiness.  
Someday, you’ll realize you’re already standing right in front of your dream.
